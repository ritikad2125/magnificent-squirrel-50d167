How to play
  • Left stick walks. Push it up to fire your jetpack. Fuel refills on the ground.
  • Right stick aims and fires automatically while held.
  • Keyboard: A/D move, W or Space jetpack, mouse to aim and shoot.
  • Grab glowing weapons and juice boxes (+50 HP) around the map.
  • +1 for every splat. −1 if you fall in the river or splat yourself.
  • First to 20 splats or highest score after 5 minutes wins!
